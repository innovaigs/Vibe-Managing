# SOURCE_AUDIT

**Deliverable 1 — Complete inventory of the source knowledge folder.**

This audit records what raw source material was studied to build the Vibe Managing Business Intelligence layer. It is an internal engineering artifact. All source-program branding, institutional names, instructor/participant names, cohort identifiers, and vendor tool names have been kept out of every *shipped* Vibe Managing asset; this audit is the one place the raw inventory is catalogued so we can trace provenance (see also `internal/PROVENANCE_MAP.md`).

## Overview

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total files inventoried | 129 |
| Content-bearing files (studied) | ~95 |
| Educational-only / discardable | ~34 |
| File types | 77 PDF, 42 DOCX, 7 XLSX, 1 DOCM |
| Source structure | 1 orientation unit, 9 sequential modules, 4 specialty clinics/workshops, 1 capstone growth-plan set, 1 logistics/bio bundle |

The source is a cohort-based small-business management program: an orientation, nine sequential modules, four specialty sessions (a financial workshop, a digital-marketing clinic, a legal clinic, a negotiation clinic), and a capstone "growth plan" produced by each business. Each unit ships as a main slide deck ("MAIN"/"PPT"), a pre-work document, a workbook of worksheets, and a class agenda.

## Content classification legend

- **CORE** — primary business intelligence; studied in full and transformed into skills/agents.
- **REUSABLE** — templates, calculators, checklists, worked examples with reusable structure.
- **DISCARD** — exists only because this was a taught cohort program (bios, schedules, logistics, room/tech onboarding, class agendas, evaluation admin). Educational wrapper removed; no business intelligence lost.
- **PARTIAL** — mixed; educational wrapper discarded, embedded diagnostics/frameworks extracted.

## Duplication analysis

- Every workbook exists as both `.docx` and `.pdf` — the same content in two formats. Studied once per pair.
- `Live Plan Assignments` and `Growth Plan Template (Blank)` each appear in two folders (orientation/logistics duplicates). Deduplicated.
- The financial forecasting model appears in several near-identical worksheet variants (monthly, 5-year, template, worked example). Treated as one modeling structure with variants.
- Statement/ratio/forecasting content overlaps between Module 3 and the Financial Workshop. Merged into one finance knowledge domain.

## Gap analysis (topics implied by a running business but thin/absent in the source)

| Gap | Handling |
|---|---|
| Detailed sales pipeline / CRM process design | Partly implied by marketing + growth; extended as `CLAUDE-DERIVED` in the Sales skills, clearly tagged |
| Customer retention / churn mechanics | Implied by growth diagnostics; formalized as `SYNTHESIZED` |
| Detailed tax/accounting compliance | Out of scope of source; handled by escalation-to-specialist guardrails, not fabricated |
| Inventory/supply-chain depth | Light in source; kept minimal, tagged where extended |
| Data/privacy/security compliance | Not in source; represented only as guardrails + escalation, never as invented domain expertise |

Anything not grounded in the source is labelled `CLAUDE-DERIVED` or `SYNTHESIZED` in `internal/PROVENANCE_MAP.md` so source-grounded intelligence is never confused with model-added reasoning.

## Method note

Text was extracted from all PDF/DOCX/DOCM/XLSX files and studied domain-by-domain. The transformation goal throughout: *not* to summarize the material, but to ask of every framework, worksheet, formula, and lesson — **"if an AI agent had to run this part of a company, what would it need to know, ask, analyze, decide, monitor, and execute?"** — and to encode that answer as skills, agents, workflows, and schemas.
