# Human Judgment Boundary

**Deliverable 18 — Vibe Managing does not pretend humans are unnecessary.** This document draws the line explicitly and is enforced through per-skill approval/escalation sections and the control plane.

## AI can own
- Analysis and diagnosis
- Continuous monitoring and anomaly detection
- Drafting (documents, plans, messages, proposals) — for human review
- Coordination and task orchestration
- Calculations, forecasting, scenario modeling
- Research and information gathering
- Routine, reversible, low-risk workflows
- Preparation of options and recommendations with confidence levels
- Low-risk reversible execution (internal tasks, dashboards, reminders, internal-review drafts)

## Humans must own
- Company values and mission-level judgment
- Major strategy and direction
- Irreversible decisions
- Sensitive people decisions (hire, fire, discipline, compensation, performance)
- Major capital allocation and any movement of money
- Legal commitments and regulated decisions
- High-risk financial decisions
- Ethical judgment and anything affecting a specific person's livelihood

## How the boundary is enforced
1. **Risk classification** on every action (reversibility × impact).
2. **Autonomy ceilings** per skill and per action-type (`L0–L4`); irreversible/critical actions can never be auto-executed.
3. **Approval routing** for everything above the line, with a clear request (what/why/cost/reversibility/recommendation).
4. **Escalation targets** named per skill: founder, accountant/CPA, attorney, HR, executive, specialist.
5. **Transparency:** the founder can always see what the system did autonomously and undo reversible actions.

## Design intent
The goal is not to replace the founder's judgment but to remove the *complexity tax* around it — so the founder spends time on decisions that need a human, and the system absorbs the rest.
