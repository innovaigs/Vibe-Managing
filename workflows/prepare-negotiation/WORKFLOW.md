# Workflow: prepare-negotiation

Prepare the founder to negotiate well. The system plans; the human negotiates.

## 1. Understand
Capture: the parties, the deal/issues, what we want, what we know about the other side, deadlines, and constraints (budget, legal).

## 2. Diagnose / Prepare
`negotiation-preparation`:
- Rank OUR interests and estimate THEIRS (interests vs. positions).
- Mark our **BATNA** and estimate theirs; set **target** and **limit (reservation)** per issue; estimate the **ZOPA**.
- Build good/better/best option packages and a sequenced concession plan.
- Gather objective standards to cite; draft an open/reveal/hold-back script.
```
IF no viable BATNA                    → strengthen it BEFORE negotiating (don't negotiate from weakness)
IF terms touch contract/legal risk    → route to legal-liaison-agent → attorney before agreeing
IF price/terms affect margin/cash     → CFO checks the limit against the numbers
```

## 3. Plan output
A one-page prep: interests, BATNA/ZOPA, targets & limits, concession sequence, standards, and script.

## 4. Execute
- Auto (L1): produce the prep document and supporting analysis.
- The founder conducts the negotiation. Nothing is agreed by the system.

## 5. Monitor / Remember
Record the outcome vs. target/limit; capture what worked for next time.

## Guardrails
The system never commits terms or signs. Anything binding → founder, with attorney review for contract/legal terms.
